# Legal & Usage Disclaimer

## Purpose

PKM is a defensive security concept designed for **duress protection**. It enables the lawful owner of a device—such as a journalist protecting sources, an activist, a dissident, or any at-risk user facing coercion or forced handover—to irreversibly destroy their own data rather than surrender it.

PKM operates exclusively on the user's local device. It does not access, modify, or interact with any external systems, devices, or networks.
