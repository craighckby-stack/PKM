## 1. Purpose

The Panic Key Mechanism (PKM) is a defensive security concept designed for **duress protection**. It enables the lawful owner of a device—such as an investigative journalist protecting confidential sources, an activist, a dissident, or any at-risk individual facing coercion or forced physical surrender—to irreversibly destroy their own data rather than surrender it.

PKM operates exclusively on the user's local device. It does not access, modify, or interact with any external systems, devices, or networks.
