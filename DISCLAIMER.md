# Legal & Usage Disclaimer

## Purpose

PKM is a defensive security concept for **duress protection**: enabling
the holder of a device they own — a journalist protecting sources, an
activist, a dissident, any at-risk user facing coercion or forced
handover — to irreversibly destroy their own data rather than surrender
it.

PKM operates exclusively on the user's own device. It does not access,
modify, or interact with any other system, device, or network.
